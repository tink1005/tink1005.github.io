Since the docks
Our story

Twenty years of hospitality, one little window.

Sailor started with a simple idea: proper, fresh seafood shouldn't need a white tablecloth. After two decades cooking around Tasmania, we put it all into a truck — so the good stuff comes to you, wherever you are.

Everything's made to order with fresh Tassie produce wherever we can get it. No freezers full of pre-made anything — just real food, cooked when you order it, served with a smile from the window.
